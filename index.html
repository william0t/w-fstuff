How W&F Maps to Aaron's Wishlist
A direct match between your needs and our capabilities

Optimum Golf came in with a clear, well-defined wishlist — funnel map, weekly dashboard, CRM attribution, landing pages, and email automation. Every single item maps directly to a live capability inside the W&F Growth Portal. Below is a side-by-side view of each ask and exactly how we deliver it.

Ask #1 A Clear Funnel Map (First Contact → Membership) ▼
What Aaron Asked For
  • Defined stages: Traffic → Landing → Lead → NSA Booked → NSA Show → Close → Retention
  • Metrics and tracking at each stage
  • Campaign-specific landing pages
  • Clear attribution per source
W&F Growth Portal Delivers
GHL Pipelines
  • Custom pipeline stages built to match his exact funnel
  • Every contact card tracks source, stage, and conversion date
  • Opportunity value per stage = real revenue forecasting
  • Drag-and-drop board or list view — Aaron picks what he wants to see
  • Automated stage moves triggered by actions (booking, showing, buying)
Ask #2 Weekly Reporting Dashboard (CPC, CPL, CAC, Show Rate, Close Rate) ▼
What Aaron Asked For
  • Cost per click (CPC)
  • Cost per lead (CPL)
  • Cost per NSA booked
  • Customer acquisition cost (CAC)
  • Booking rate, show rate, close rate
W&F Growth Portal Delivers
GHL Reporting + Ads Integration
  • Custom dashboard widgets for every metric he listed
  • Google Ads + Meta Ads directly connected — CPC/CPL pulled automatically
  • Funnel conversion rates calculated in real-time
  • Weekly email digest delivered to Aaron automatically
  • We build the dashboard, he reads the numbers
Ask #3 CRM & Attribution Tracking ▼
What Aaron Asked For
  • CRM that tracks leads from first click through sale
  • Landing pages connected directly to CRM
  • Integrated booking tied to contact records
  • Real conversion tracking and funnel visibility
W&F Growth Portal Delivers
GHL CRM + UTM Tracking
  • Every lead tagged with UTM source, medium, and campaign from first click
  • Complete contact timeline: ad click → form fill → booking → show → sale
  • Booking tied directly to contact record — no manual matching
  • Replaces both Constant Contact and USchedule in one platform
  • Aaron sees exactly what ad brought in each member
Ask #4 Campaign-Specific Landing Pages ▼
What Aaron Asked For
  • Each campaign gets its own landing page
  • Single clear offer per page
  • Lead capture form + booking integration
  • Meta Pixel + Google Tag tracking built in
W&F Growth Portal Delivers
GHL Funnel Builder
  • Built-in funnel/landing page builder — no Squarespace needed
  • Each page connects natively to the CRM (zero data loss)
  • Booking calendar embedded directly on the page
  • Meta Pixel and Google Tag fire automatically on form submit and booking
  • A/B testing built in to optimize conversion
Ask #5 Email Automation — Nurture, NSA, No-Show, Reactivation ▼
What Aaron Asked For
  • Lead nurture sequence (value + credibility + CTA to book)
  • NSA booked confirmation + reminders
  • No-show reactivation sequence
  • Cold lead reactivation
W&F Growth Portal Delivers
GHL Workflows (Email + SMS)
  • All 4 sequences built and ready to customize in the portal
  • Email AND SMS — double the touchpoints, higher show rates
  • Missed call text-back: lead calls, no answer, they get a text immediately
  • Sequences trigger automatically based on pipeline stage changes
  • Completely replaces Constant Contact — no manual sends
Bonus Two-Way SMS + Unified Inbox ▼
The Current Gap
  • No centralized inbox — leads go to email, phone, DMs, separate tools
  • No way to have a real conversation with a lead in the CRM
  • Staff responding from personal phones = zero tracking
W&F Growth Portal Delivers
GHL Conversations Inbox
  • All channels in one inbox: Email, SMS, FB/IG DMs, Google Messages
  • Two-way SMS from a dedicated local number
  • Missed call text-back fires in seconds — never lose a hot lead
  • AI conversation assistant available for after-hours replies
  • Every reply tied to the contact record automatically
